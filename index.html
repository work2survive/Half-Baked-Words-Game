📖 How to Play

1. Daily Tray: Every day, all players get the exact same 16 fresh cookie letters!

2. Bake Words: Drag or tap cookies to form valid English words (4+ letters). Click Bake Word ✓ to score points!

3. The Twist — Bite 3 Cookies: Each time you bake a word, you must take 3 bites from your tray!

4. Nibble & Reshape: Some letters disappear when bitten. But others can be nibbled into new letters or split in half!

5. Free Rotations: Letters like M ↔ W and N ↔ Z can be rotated anytime for free!

6. Game Over: When no more valid words can be made from your remaining cookies, the oven cools and final score is tallied!
